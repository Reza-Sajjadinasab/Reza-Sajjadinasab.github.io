Research

Reinforcement learning for driving

Self-driving AI usually learns by copying human drivers. I build methods that let it keep improving through its own practice without forgetting what it already knew, so it stays safe in situations it has never seen. It reaches the level of the best published camera-only systems, trained on a modest research budget.

with Prof. Eshed Ohn-Bar

Machine learning for compilers

A compiler turns code into a working program, and its settings change how small or fast that program is. I build AI that predicts the right settings for each program, so its very first guess already beats the compiler's standard choice, saving many slow trial builds.

with Prof. Martin Herbordt and Red Hat

Greg: A Guided Recipe for Efficient Reinforcement Fine-Tuning in Sensorimotor Driving
R. Sajjadinasab, E. Ohn-Bar
Under review2026
Greg starts from a driving policy learned from demonstrations and keeps improving it with reinforcement learning: critic uncertainty decides what to learn from, a learned world model imagines future returns, and a KL regularizer keeps the policy close to what it already knew. It raises the Bench2Drive driving score from 84.28 to 92.03.

Published

A Graph-Based Algorithm for Optimizing GCC Compiler Flag Settings
R. Sajjadinasab, S. Arora, U. Drepper, A. Sanaullah, M. Herbordt
IEEE HPEC2024 · Paper
Treats compiler flags as a graph: experiments reward flag pairs that help, and a cut-off keeps the strongest ones, giving 8–13% smaller code than GCC’s -Oz.
Further Optimizations and Analysis of Smith-Waterman with Vector Extensions
R. Sajjadinasab, H. Rastaghi, H. Shahzad, S. Arora, U. Drepper, M. Herbordt
IEEE IPDPSW2024 · Paper
Speeds up Smith–Waterman sequence alignment with vector instructions by reorganizing memory along diagonals and packing work into SIMD vectors, about 6× faster than a naive version.
Modeling the Effect of Automated and Human-Driven Vehicles on the Performance of Intelligent Intersections
H. Mirzahossein, R. Sajjadinasab, I. Gholampour, A. Zamani
ASCE J. Transportation Engineering, Part A150(6), 2024 · Paper
Models how mixes of human-driven, highly automated and connected vehicles change the capacity of intelligent intersections, using a different following gap for each pair of vehicle types.
A Hybrid Deep and Machine Learning Model for Short-Term Traffic Volume Forecasting of Adjacent Intersections
H. Mirzahossein, I. Gholampour, R. Sajjadinasab, A. Zamani
IET Intelligent Transport Systems16(11), 2022 · Paper
Forecasts short-term traffic at three neighboring intersections from loop-detector data, combining wavelet denoising, GRUs and LSTMs, with over 94% accuracy.
